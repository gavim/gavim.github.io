Playing a little

How do I play?

Swipe on the board to slide the tiles. Two equal numbers merge once per move. A new 2 or 4 appears only when the board changes. Reach 2048, then keep playing to meet more friends.

What is Glide play?

Open Your little corner using the settings button, then choose Glide play under Move your way. Keep your finger down and make deliberate strokes, pausing or turning between moves. A long straight stroke or holding still does not repeat moves.

How do undo and saving work?

Undo restores one previous move, including the tile that appeared. Autosave remembers your current game. Save game creates a separate, single bookmark; Load game restores it. Changing board sizes or starting a new game does not overwrite that bookmark.

Can I choose a different board?

Your little corner offers 3 by 3, 4 by 4, and 5 by 5 boards. Changing size starts a new board, with confirmation if you have already made moves. Each size keeps its own best score.

Does progress sync between devices?

There is no in-app account or cross-device sync. Games and preferences stay on your device and may be included in device-managed backups. Deleting the app removes its local progress, so do not delete it as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your game.

What accessibility controls are available?

The board has VoiceOver actions for moving up, down, left, and right. Connected keyboards can use arrow keys. Animations respect Reduce Motion, and you can turn haptics off in Your little corner.

No ads. No timers. No internet needed to play.
