Support Privacy policy

Privacy policy

This policy describes the ivy 2048 app for iPhone and iPad.

Last updated: November 8, 2026.

No data collection

ivy 2048 does not collect data or send it to the developer or third parties.

There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or network requests. The app does not access your photos, contacts, or location.

Your game stays on your device

Your board, quick save, best scores, and preferences are stored on this device. They may be included in backups managed by your device.

The app does not provide account-based or cross-device syncing. The developer cannot access or restore your saved game.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its local game data. Any backup copies are managed through your device's backup settings.

Contacting support

The app does not automatically send support messages or diagnostic reports. If you choose to contact support outside the app, the recipient receives the information you choose to send. Please do not include sensitive information.

For support or questions about this policy, email gavinlivy@gmail.com. You can also visit the Support page.

About this website

This static website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded third-party content. Visiting the website is separate from using the offline app.

GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details about that hosting service.
